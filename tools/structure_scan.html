    Scan rules

    Swings need two completed candles on each side. A close beyond a swing establishes or continues direction (BOS). A break against the previous direction is CHoCH. A three-candle FVG must form on the break candle or within the next two candles. The proposed entry is its midpoint, awaiting a retracement. A later midpoint touch consumes that setup. No matching untouched gap means no entry. Swing levels are close-confirmation thresholds, not entry orders. Analysis is a snapshot; run again for updated levels. No orders are placed.
