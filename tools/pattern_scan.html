How does this pattern work?
  1. Watch the usual price area. We use the highest and lowest prices from the last 20 completed one-minute price bars.
  2. Look for a move outside that area. The move must exceed twice the buy/sell price gap (the spread).
  3. Watch for a quick return. That market must return inside its own range within 90 seconds. Each market is checked independently.

A match creates a research alert for a possible reversal. It does not predict a certain price move. Price updates trigger the checks; the scanner does not wait for a candle to close.
